# The 20-week plan (≈ 4.5 months)

This plan is built for one goal: **an offer within 4–5 months**. It assumes about **3 hours on weekdays and 6–7 hours on weekend days** (~28 h/week). If you have less time, stretch each week to 9–10 days — don't skip weeks.

> **Three rules that matter more than any module**
> 1. **DSA every single day** — 2 problems minimum (one new pattern, one revision). Coding rounds are the gate to every interview loop.
> 2. **Learn actively** — before opening an interview answer, say your own answer out loud. Explaining is the skill being tested.
> 3. **Track honestly** — mark a module complete only when you can answer its questions without looking. Flag the rest for revision.

## How every module is structured

Each module goes from zero to interview-ready:

1. **Start here — the basics**: plain-language explanation for someone new to the topic, with analogies and small examples.
2. **The deep dive**: internals, production-grade code, trade-offs and pitfalls.
3. **Quick check questions**.
4. **Questions asked at top companies**: frequently reported questions from Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Meta, Flipkart, Uber and others (tags reflect commonly reported interview experiences, not official question lists).
5. **End-to-end scenarios**: longer "you're on call / design this / debug this" questions that combine everything in the module.

## Daily template (weekday, ~3 hours)

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 45 min | **New DSA problem** on the week's pattern. 20 min thinking; if stuck, read the hint/editorial, then close it and **re-code from scratch**. |
| 25 min | **Revision problem** solved 3, 7 or 21 days ago. |
| 75 min | **One module section** in this app (use the 🍅 timer: 3 × 25 min). Answer questions out loud. |
| 15 min | Write 5 lines in the module's **Notes**: what surprised you, and one sentence you'd say in an interview. |

Weekends: finish the week's modules, do a **timed set of 3 problems in 90 minutes**, and (from week 9) one LeetCode/Codeforces contest.

## Milestones

| By end of | You should be able to |
|---|---|
| Week 4 | Explain JVM memory, HashMap, threads basics; solve easy array/string problems in < 15 min |
| Week 8 | Build & explain a Spring Boot REST + JPA service with JWT; solve 60% of mediums in < 30 min |
| Week 12 | Explain Kafka, sagas, Kubernetes basics; draw a URL-shortener design with numbers |
| Week 16 | Finish 300+ problems, 6+ designs, 8 STAR stories; pass mock interviews |
| Weeks 17–20 | Interview loops and offers |

## Problem count targets

| Target | Count | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum to be credible | 250 | 60 easy / 160 medium / 30 hard |
| Comfortable at FAANG | 400 | 80 / 250 / 70 |

Use the **Coding Q&A** track for curated problems with full solutions, and the DSA track's must-solve lists.

## When you fall behind

You will. Don't restart — **cut scope instead**: keep DSA daily, keep the System Design and Spring modules, and move CP and Level 4 extras to "after interviews". An offer comes from consistency, not from finishing every page.
